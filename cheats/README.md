## The five stops

### The farm (eggs and butter) · top left

- Talk to Keelan and say you're ready. Run into a hen to pick it up, then carry it to the gate at the bottom of the pen. One hen at a time.
- You win when all 20 are in. You lose if 9 are loose at once.
- **Make it easy:** find the cupcake first (see secrets) and give it to Keelan. He starts catching hens too, and it becomes very hard to lose.

### The Birkie (flour) · top right

- Talk to John under the arch.
- Left and right change lanes. Up, A or Space jumps fallen skiers.
- Grab a cup at each of the three feed stations (be in the cup's lane). That's your energy.
- At Highway OO, ski through the banana for a boost.
- On the lake the wind pushes you a lane sideways. Steer back.
- Stay ahead of the sweep snowmobile (the red dot on the left of the screen).

### Vancouver (chocolate) · bottom left

- Walk onto the bridge. Go straight down the road through the park and into downtown. **REALLY GOOD PHO** is on the right at the second cross street. Enter there.
- Pick any outfit. Then, on the rooftops, switch between the three with **1, 2, 3** (or tap a face):
  - **Joe:** sonar (shows hidden tripwires) and Doc Alex's gadget
  - **Lars:** sprint
  - **John:** lifts things
- **Roof 1:** John lifts the log that blocks the way. Walk up to Doc Alex for the briefing. Then John lays the log across the gap.
- **Roof 2:** Lars sprints from cover to cover to the LIGHT POWER switch. The lights go out for 20 seconds: grab the key from under the parked spotlight at the bottom, and use it on the gate across the plank.
- **Roof 3:** stay left of the guard's flashlight. Joe's sonar marks the invisible lasers. At the laser panel a pattern of squares flashes: light the same ones from memory, three rounds (5×5, then 6×6, then 7×7). A phone photo of the screen works.
- **The Confectioner:** aim the gadget and zap him. Candy canes stun you, so keep moving.
- Pick up the chocolate, then head for the blinking EXIT sign.
- **Lars's anagrams:** 60 seconds, longer words score far more (a 6-letter word is worth twenty 3-letter words). You get the chocolate whether you win or lose. If Lars wins, Joe carries it.

### Fargo (sugar) · bottom right

- Get in the CR-V and drive to **Lil Q's**, the orange station on the right side of town.
- **The scooter race:** two laps, three checkpoints each. Roads are fast, grass is slow, the dirt paths are shortcuts, and orange arrows on the road give a boost.
- **The snack shop:** Alex wants **Ginger Ale** and **Mott's Berry Gummies**.
- **Jackie** won't help until she gets plain pasta. Drive to the **diner** (top left of town) and ask Deb for "Plain pasta to go".

### Wylee Woods (milk) · straight down from the kitchen

- Five holes. You need to beat Jack and at least tie Margie.
- A or Space stops the aim arrow, then again to stop the power bar.
- Long throws curve left at the end, so aim a little right. Inside the dotted circle it's a putt and flies straight.
- Trees stop the disc. Water and out of bounds cost a stroke.
- Take the practice throw. It doesn't count.

## The kitchen

Once all five are in the basket, go home and walk up to the counter.

| Step | The right amount |
|---|---|
| Butter | 1 cup |
| Sugar | ¾ cup |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Flour | 2¼ cups (it pours fast) |
| Scoop | 6 dough balls: tap when the scoop is over a spot |
| Chocolate chips | copy the pattern on the card |
| Sprinkles | only if you found Brian: shake them on |
| Bake | take them out at **10:00**, give or take 30 seconds (about 8 real seconds) |

For the pours, hold to pour and let go on the gold line. Too much or too little and you just try that one again.

## All seven secrets

Find all seven for the Cookie Legend crown and the secret note.

| Secret | Where |
|---|---|
| **Cupcake** | Behind the left side of the barn. Follow the faint track that leaves the barn path and loops around the back. Its frosting pokes up over the roof. |
| **Sprinkles** | Talk to Brian, out in his boat on the pond at the top of the farm. |
| **Lucky clover** | Logan, in the clover patch left of the farm path. Name the album 8 times in a row (answers below). |
| **Cutting board** | Jack C, in the meadow just above the kitchen. Make a board with him: saw on the pencil line (twice; the saw skips at the knot on the second cut), lay the five strips in the order on his drawing, plane left-right following the arrow without rushing, then hold to oil and let go in the green. Maureen and Ethan mention it at the taste test. |
| **Headband** | In the snow just above the log cabin at the Birkie, on the right. |
| **Carbon shoes** | In the grass on the right of the road down to Wylee Woods, about a third of the way. |
| **Ice cream** | On the first rooftop of the heist, pick "Lars: long-jump it" at the gap. He finds a pint in the gutter. |
